## 📌 About the Project

**Virasat – Roots & Radiance** is a digital platform designed to showcase and preserve India's rich cultural heritage and traditions.

The project aims to make India's heritage more accessible through **digital storytelling, interactive maps, AI-powered assistance, and multilingual information**.

Our solution combines technology with cultural heritage to help users explore historical places, understand their importance, and connect with India's traditions in an engaging way.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

India has a vast and diverse cultural heritage, including:

* Historical monuments
* Forts and heritage sites
* Traditional arts and crafts
* Local traditions
* Cultural stories
* Regional history

However, information about many heritage locations and traditions is scattered across different sources and is not always presented in an interactive and easily accessible way.

**Virasat** aims to provide a centralized digital experience for discovering and learning about India's cultural heritage.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

Our solution focuses on:

* 🌏 Digital showcasing of India's cultural heritage
* 🗺️ Geo-tagged heritage site mapping
* 📖 AI-powered cultural storytelling
* 🤖 Interactive AI chatbot assistance
* 🗣️ Hindi and English voice assistance
* 📱 Cross-platform accessibility
* 📴 Offline caching for areas with low connectivity

The core idea is to **showcase India's rich cultural legacy through digital technology**.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Heritage Mapping

Users can explore heritage locations using geo-tagged information and maps.

### 📚 Digital Storytelling

AI-assisted storytelling can provide engaging information about historical and cultural sites.

### 🤖 AI Chatbot

An interactive chatbot can guide users and answer questions related to heritage locations.

### 🗣️ Bilingual AI Voice Assistant

The platform is designed to provide information in **Hindi and English**, making heritage information more accessible.

### 📴 Offline Support

Offline caching can help users access important information in areas with poor internet connectivity.

### 👥 Community Participation

Community moderation and heritage experts can help maintain the accuracy of crowdsourced information.

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

| Component        | Technology                         |
| ---------------- | ---------------------------------- |
| Frontend         | Flutter                            |
| Backend          | Node.js, Python, FastAPI           |
| Database         | PostgreSQL                         |
| Geo-Mapping      | PostGIS                            |
| AI & NLP         | Fine-tuned Large Language Models   |
| Voice Assistance | AI-based bilingual voice assistant |

The technical approach in our SIH presentation specifies Flutter for cross-platform development, Node.js/Python with FastAPI for the backend, PostgreSQL with PostGIS for geo-tagged mapping, and fine-tuned LLMs for storytelling and chatbot guidance.

## 🔐 Feasibility & Scalability

The project is designed using mature and relatively low-cost technologies.

The proposed approach can begin with an MVP and initially be tested at **2–3 heritage sites before scaling**.

### Challenges

* Accuracy of crowdsourced content
* Internet connectivity in remote heritage locations
* Onboarding non-technical local artisans

### Proposed Strategies

* Community moderation with heritage experts
* Offline caching
* Bilingual AI voice assistant
* Use of open heritage datasets and open-source technologies
